# Submission

## failed_boundary

The failure is at the provider-response validation boundary in `StripeTransfers.create`, after the ledger intent/reservation has committed and after `transport.send(request)` returned, but before a receipt is returned to `pay` and before `ledger.confirm`. `pay` orders those operations as `prepare` (line 48), `create` (line 52), then `confirm` (line 53) in `/workspace/app/core.py`. The recorded traceback identifies `/workspace/app/adapters.py:122`, the `NeedsReconciliation("Response does not match the payment")` branch. Thus this was not an SQLite confirmation/posting failure and not the timeout branch. The exact response field (object, amount, currency, destination, or transfer_group) that differed is not retained in the incident artifacts; validation compares that complete tuple at `/workspace/app/adapters.py:119-122`.

## evidence

The approved input is documented as `milestone-42`, `acct_demo_contractor`, 50000 cents, at `/workspace/incident/report.md:3-11`. `/workspace/incident/stderr.txt:1-10` reports that exact mismatch error and traceback through `main.py:51`, `core.py:52`, and `adapters.py:122`; rerunning the supplied invocation produced the same exit-1 traceback in `/workspace/incident/reproductions/01/stderr.txt` (and `reproduce_incident` reported exit status 1). It also proves that the expected response tuple did not compare equal. The outbound mapping is deterministic: POST `/v1/transfers`, amount 50000, USD, the stated destination/group, and idempotency key `contractor-payment:milestone-42` (`/workspace/app/adapters.py:105-114`; the same expected request is shown in `/workspace/app/fixtures/success.json:3-10`). The intent/reservation behavior is implemented in `/workspace/app/adapters.py:44-61`, while posting, balance move, and receipt save occur only in `confirm` at lines 63-79.

## external_outcome

Known only at the local boundary: a configured transport supplied what the application treated as an HTTP-200 response, but its payment-identifying body did not match the durable requested payment. That is insufficient to accept a `tr_...` receipt for this payment. No transfer id was accepted or persisted. It does not establish that the actual remote provider accepted the requested transfer, rejected it, accepted a different transfer, or that the returned response was stale/corrupt/misrouted; the provider transport configuration and the mismatching body are absent (`incident/report.md:13-16`), and no HTTP trace artifact was produced. It also cannot establish bank settlement; even a valid provider transfer receipt would not do that under the documented semantics (`/workspace/README.md:17-19`).
